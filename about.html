About Me

Hello! I'm Jason Luis, a Cloud Computing student at Santa Monica College who enjoys learning, building, and solving problems with technology.

My Background

I'm currently pursuing my associate degree in Cloud Computing at Santa Monica College. I'm interested in understanding how computer systems, networks, and cloud services work together, and I enjoy putting what I learn into practice.

Through my coursework, I've gained hands-on experience with Amazon Web Services (AWS), Ubuntu Linux, Windows Server, Python, C++, and web development. I've deployed an AWS EC2 server with Pi-hole and WireGuard VPN, configured Amazon VPC networking, hosted static websites using Amazon S3, and built programming projects in Python and C++.

Alongside school, I bring more than seven years of customer service experience. Working with customers and teammates has helped me strengthen my communication, organization, and problem-solving skills.

My Goals

I want to keep improving my technical skills, work toward industry certifications, and gain experience through internships or entry-level opportunities in cloud computing, IT, and software development. I'm especially interested in opportunities where I can learn from others and contribute to real technical projects.
